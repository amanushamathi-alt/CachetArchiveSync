Ran a broad regex sweep (password=, pwd=, apikey, secret assignment, application-key-like long
tokens) across every appsettings*.json under src/ - zero hits. Also inspected the new
env.local.ps1.example line by line:

  ConnectionStrings__CachetArchiveSync = "Server=(localdb)\MSSQLLocalDB;Database=CachetArchiveSync;Trusted_Connection=True;TrustServerCertificate=True;"
  B2__SystemKeyId = "REPLACE_WITH_YOUR_B2_SYSTEM_KEY_ID"
  B2__SystemApplicationKey = "REPLACE_WITH_YOUR_B2_SYSTEM_APPLICATION_KEY"
  B2__BucketName = "REPLACE_WITH_YOUR_B2_BUCKET_NAME"

The connection string uses Trusted_Connection=True (Windows Integrated Auth) - no embedded
username/password, and points at a purely local (localdb) instance, so it is not a credential.

placeholder-that-looks-real secret was found anywhere in the checked-in tree.
